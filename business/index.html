How it works

No ads, no feeds. Only real groups who are already considering you.

1

A group lists your place

Friends ask each other “where should we go?” and type the places they’re thinking of. If yours is one, and they’ve asked for offers, you’re in.

2

You get a request

“A group of 6 is choosing between you and 2 other places.” Send an offer in one tap, or write one for them. Or skip it, no obligation.

3

They pick you, and show a code

If they choose your place, the group gets one code. You check it in one tap when they arrive. It works once.

Questions

Do I have to send an offer every time?

No. You decide for each group. Skip any request you don’t want.

How do groups find my place?

They type the places they’re considering. If they type your name and ask for offers, you get a request, so use the name your customers know you by.

Who sees my offer?

Only that group, on your answer in their question. Other places never see your offers.

What does it cost?

Nothing during launch. Later, a small fee only when a group uses your code. You’ll be told well in advance, and you can stop any time.

How do I check a code?

On your business page: type the code a group shows you and tap Check. Each code works once.
