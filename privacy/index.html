Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Sway is a private app for asking your friends before you decide. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the app, and the choices you have. We don’t sell your data and we don’t show ads.

The short version. Only the people in your circles see what you post there. We keep what’s needed to run the app, plus simple usage numbers and error reports in our own database. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

What we collect

  • Account: your first name, and your email and password if you add them (passwords are stored encrypted by our sign-in provider; we never see them). Guests have only a name.
  • What you create: circles, questions, answers and photos you upload, votes, comments, nudges, starred circles and your settings (such as notification choices and blocked people).
  • Notifications: if you switch them on, a technical address for your device that lets us send notifications to it.
  • Usage numbers: which actions happen in the app and when (for example “a question was asked” or “a vote was cast”), linked to your account, so we can see whether the app is working and improve it. We record the type of action, not what you wrote.
  • Error reports: when something breaks, the error message, the page and your browser type.
  • Reports: if you report a question or comment, the report and what it was about.
  • Businesses: if you apply as a business, its name, type, city, address, website, phone and your contact name and email, plus the offers you send and the codes you check.

Who can see what

  • Questions, votes and comments are visible only to members of the circles they were asked in.
  • If a question uses anonymous voting, or you vote privately, nobody else sees what you picked; only totals are shown. If you ask with “hide that I asked”, other members don’t see that it was you.
  • Businesses see only the size of a group, how many places it’s comparing and when it decides. They never see names, questions, comments or votes.
  • Our team can access data when needed to run the app, investigate a report, or keep people safe.

Why we use it

To provide the app (showing your questions to your circles, counting votes, sending the notifications you asked for), to keep it safe (handling reports and blocks, preventing abuse), and to understand and improve it (usage numbers and error reports). Where the law requires a legal basis, these are: providing the service you asked for, our legitimate interest in keeping it safe and working, and your consent for notifications.

Who helps us run Sway

  • Supabase stores our database and handles sign-in.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts provides the typeface; your browser loads it from Google.
  • Apple, Google and Mozilla deliver notifications to your device if you switch them on.

They process data only to provide their service to us. Some of them may process data outside your country, under their own safeguards.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and what you created: until you delete them, or your account.
  • Usage numbers: up to 13 months. Error reports: up to 90 days.
  • Reports: as long as needed to handle them and keep the app safe.

Your choices

  • Delete your account any time: You tab → Delete account. Circles you made pass to the member who joined first, so your friends don’t lose them.
  • Switch notifications off, mute circles, or choose whose activity notifies you (You tab).
  • Block people and report content.

Children

Sway isn’t for children under 13, or under the age of digital consent where you live (16 in some countries) without a parent’s permission.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we’ll tell you in the app before it applies.
