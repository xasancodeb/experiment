# Testing with 5 friends

The cheapest way to find what's confusing: watch five people use the app. Five finds most of the
big problems. About 15 minutes each.

## Before you start

- Use people who haven't seen the app yet, on **their own phone**.
- Say: *"I'm testing the app, not you. Please think out loud: say what you're looking for and
  what you expect to happen. There are no wrong answers."*
- Then **stay quiet**. Don't help, don't explain, don't point. If they're stuck for a minute,
  ask *"What would you try next?"* Every time you want to help is a problem worth fixing.
- Write down what they **do and say**, not what you think they meant.

## The tasks

Give them one at a time, in your own words.

1. **Join.** Send them an invite link to a circle (or a question link). *"A friend sent you
   this. Get in and vote."*
   Watch: do they understand what the app is from the first screen? Do they choose "Just vote"?
2. **Vote.** *"Answer the question waiting for you."*
   Watch: how long it takes, whether they find the comments, whether "Vote anonymously" makes
   sense.
3. **Ask.** *"Ask your friends something you're actually deciding this week."*
   Watch: do they find Ask? Do they choose who to ask without help? Do they notice the options
   (deadline, anonymous)?
4. **Find the result.** Close the question for them (or wait), then: *"Find out what your
   friends decided."*
   Watch: do they notice the notification, the badge, or the result card?
5. **Make a circle and invite.** *"Make a group for your family and invite one person."*
   Watch: do they find Circles? Can they send the link?

## After the tasks, ask

- *"In one sentence, what is this app for?"*
- *"What was the most confusing moment?"*
- *"Would you use it with your friends? What would stop you?"*
- *"If you could change one thing, what would it be?"*

## What to look for

- Moments they **hesitate** for more than a few seconds or tap the wrong thing.
- Words they **misread** (write down the exact word).
- Things they **expected** that didn't happen.
- What they **smile** at: that's what to make more of.

## After all five

List every problem, count how many people hit it, and fix the ones **two or more** people hit
first. Send me the list and I'll make the changes.
